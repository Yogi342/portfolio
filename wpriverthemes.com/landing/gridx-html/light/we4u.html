We4U - Contemporary USA Women's Fashion & Lifestyle Brand

We4U is an American women's apparel brand designed for the modern woman seeking timeless silhouettes, breathable linen essentials, tailored blazers, and chic evening dresses. With target demographics concentrated in major US style hubs (California, New York, Florida, Texas), the brand needed an editorial digital storefront that communicated luxury and effortless sophistication while sustaining high-velocity mobile conversion rates.

As the frontend developer and e-commerce specialist for this project, I engineered the entire storefront experience on Shopify. This encompassed architecting custom Liquid sections, designing a dynamic product detail page (PDP) with color swatches and US size calculators, coding an AJAX slide-out cart drawer with real-time free shipping indicators, and overseeing the comprehensive store management workflow from SKU structuring to nationwide fulfillment configuration.

Store Operations & Technical Implementations

  • Editorial Collection Lookbooks: Built dynamic Shopify 2.0 section templates allowing the merchandising team to release seasonal fashion drops ("Spring Linen Edit", "California Sunset", "Resort Wear") with drag-and-drop lookbooks and quick-buy hotspots.
  • High-Conversion Slide-out Bag: Created an AJAX drawer featuring real-time shipping threshold meters ("Add $15 to unlock Free Standard US Shipping"), cross-sell accessories, and one-tap Apple Pay/Shop Pay acceleration.
  • Dynamic Sizing & Color Swatch Switcher: Implemented responsive variant selectors with live image synchronization, interactive US sizing conversion tables (inches & cm), and back-in-stock Klaviyo email capture for out-of-stock sizes.
  • Complete Store & Catalog Management: Structured high-volume SKU hierarchies across colors, sizes, and fabric care attributes; configured automated collection rules; and managed multi-location inventory synchronization across West and East Coast 3PL fulfillment hubs.
  • US Sales Tax & Regional Compliance: Configured Shopify Tax to handle state-by-state economic nexus rules and automated destination-based sales tax calculations across all 50 US states.

Luxury Editorial Look

Crafted an editorial layout reminiscent of Vogue and Harper's Bazaar. Clean serif typography (Playfair Display) paired with neutral sand and terracotta tones gives the storefront an elevated, high-fashion pedigree.

Mobile-First Architecture

With over 78% of US fashion shoppers on smartphones, I designed sticky bottom action bars, touch-friendly swipeable lookbooks, and instant cart updates with zero layout shift (CLS < 0.05).

Scalable Store Operations

Engineered backend CSV catalog import routines, automated Shopify Flow tags for VIP customers and pre-orders, and established standardized product description templates with fabric care guides.
