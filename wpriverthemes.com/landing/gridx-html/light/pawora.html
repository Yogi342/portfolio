Shopify E-Commerce & Store Management

Pawora Pet Store

Pawora - Premium Pet Supplies & Accessories

Pawora is a modern, high-converting pet e-commerce brand built on the Shopify 2.0 platform. Designed to provide a delightful and frictionless shopping experience for pet owners, the store features lifestyle-driven visuals, clear product value propositions, and seamless navigation.

As the developer and store manager, I was responsible for custom theme customization, responsive product pages, interactive cart drawers, variant switchers, category-specific navigation, and full-spectrum Shopify store management including catalog organization, inventory synchronization, and checkout optimization.

  • Platform

    Shopify 2.0

  • Role

    Shopify Developer & Store Manager

  • Category

    Pet Care & E-Commerce

  • Key Tech

    Liquid, HTML5/SCSS, JS

Core Features & Store Operations

  • Custom Theme Setup & Liquid Customization: Tailored section schemas, custom banners, trust badges, and mobile-first responsive styling.
  • Catalog & Variant Management: Configured comprehensive product matrices with multi-size, color, and breed variant selectors.
  • Conversion & Cart Optimization: Integrated slide-out AJAX cart drawer, sticky Add-to-Cart, customer reviews, and fast one-page checkout.
  • Apps & Operations: Integrated shipping calculators, currency switchers, automated email notifications, and inventory tracking.
